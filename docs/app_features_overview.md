# Ustadia App Features Overview

Ustadia is a mobile learning app for students. It combines English lessons, practice tasks, AI help, teacher assignments, meetings, exams, and progress tracking in one place.

## Main Features

### Onboarding and Language
New users can choose the app language during onboarding. The app supports English, Russian, and Uzbek, and the selected language is used across the interface.

### Dashboard
The dashboard is the student’s home screen. It shows quick access to today’s plan, lessons, practice, assignments, mock exams, reels, meetings, leaderboard, and profile progress.

### Today’s Plan
Students can continue the recommended lesson or task directly from the home screen. This helps them know what to study next without searching.

### Lessons
Students can study structured lesson units from system lessons or from a selected teacher. Lessons can include reading, listening, grammar, writing, quizzes, flashcards, and other interactive activities.

### Teacher Selection
Students can choose a teacher from settings. When a teacher is selected, the app can show teacher-related lessons and assignments. Students can also return to system lessons.

### English Level
Students can view their current English level in the profile. When using system lessons, they can change their level to study content that fits them better.

### Quizzes and Section Tasks
Students can answer different question types such as single choice, multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blank, short answer, matching, and ordering tasks. They can move between questions, return to previous ones, and see which questions are completed.

### Answer Review
After submitting answers, students can review correct answers. In reading and listening tasks, the app can also show answer evidence, such as highlighted text or audio time ranges when available.

### Reading, Grammar, Listening, and Writing
Reading and grammar sections show learning content before tasks. Listening sections can include audio and transcript-based review. Writing sections support typed answers and file uploads when required.

### Translation Helper
Students can long-press or select text anywhere in the app to translate it. Translation supports Uzbek and Russian, and students can change the translation language from the translation menu.

### Practice
The Practice area contains short exercises for daily training:

- Flashcard Sprint: review vocabulary, flip cards, mark known words, and hear pronunciation.
- Word Match: match words with their meanings.
- Build the Sentence: put words in the correct order.
- Listen & Tap: train listening recognition with audio.
- Monkey Type: improve typing speed, accuracy, and focus.

### Ask AI
Students can speak or type with the AI assistant. The voice agent supports microphone interaction, text messages, animated voice feedback, and speaker/phone audio output modes.

### Assignments
Teacher-assigned tasks help students practice and consolidate knowledge. Students can open assignment sections, complete tasks, and track progress.

### Mock Exams
Students can take IELTS-style mock exams, complete exam sections, track remaining time, and view results, scores, and exam history.

### Reels
Students can watch short educational videos, like posts, comment, and open author profiles to see more reels from the same creator.

### Meets
Students can view upcoming online meetings and meeting history. Meeting links open in the browser or supported apps such as Google Meet or Zoom.

### Notifications
Students can receive notifications and open the notifications page from the app. Teacher invitations and learning updates can be managed there when available.

### Leaderboard
Students can see weekly ranking and XP progress. This helps motivate regular learning and compare progress with other students.

### Profile and Statistics
The profile shows student information, XP, current level, completed tasks, vocabulary progress, and learning breakdown.

### Account Settings
Students can edit account information, change profile photo, change app language, choose teacher, log out, or delete the account.

### Guided Tutorial
New users can optionally start an interactive tutorial. It explains important pages, buttons, and learning flows step by step with animated guidance.

## Client Summary

Ustadia gives students a complete learning experience: lessons, practice exercises, AI voice support, translation, teacher assignments, mock exams, meetings, reels, progress tracking, profile management, and guided onboarding.
