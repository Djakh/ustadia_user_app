# Google Play Families safety review

## Purpose and audience

Ustadia User is a private English-learning app. Students use teacher-assigned lessons, IELTS practice, AI English tutoring, and learning reels. The app is not a social networking product and does not provide student publishing tools.

## IELTS Writing submissions

- The upload entry point is `WritingSectionPage` (`lib/features/common/presentation/pages/sections_types/writing_section/writing_section_page.dart`).
- A blocking notice is shown immediately before the native file picker. It cannot be dismissed by tapping outside or using back; the user must cancel or explicitly continue.
- The notice tells students to submit only the required document/image, omit addresses, phone numbers, passwords, IDs, financial information, and private photos, and explains that submissions are privately reviewed by authorized teachers/administrators and not shown to other students.
- `LearnWritingBottomSheet` hides the paper-upload option when the server sends `social_permissions.can_submit_ielts_writing: false`. Missing/null permission data preserves the existing flow for backward compatibility; it is not treated as proof of authorization.
- The existing upload endpoint is `/uploads`. Server-side authorization and storage visibility must still be enforced by the backend.

## Profile avatars

- The avatar entry point is `ProfileEditableAvatar`.
- A separate blocking notice appears before camera/gallery selection and warns that the image may be visible to other users and must not contain IDs, addresses, phone numbers, school details, financial information, private messages, or other sensitive data.
- Existing image picker validation and `imageQuality: 90` behavior are preserved. EXIF stripping is not currently implemented; the app does not request location permission.
- `social_permissions.can_upload_public_avatar: false` hides the edit affordance. Null leaves existing behavior until the backend supplies a definitive permission.
- A “Report profile image” action is available on another user’s reel profile. The mobile backend currently exposes no report endpoint, so the action collects a reason and truthfully explains that reporting is unavailable and support must be contacted. No fake request or success state is used.

## Roles, age, and permissions

The profile response currently exposes `roles` and `currentTeacher`; no parent/child relationship, age flag, or verified-adult field was found in this mobile repository. The optional `social_permissions` object is parsed for the two upload capabilities above, but server enforcement and admin controls are required before those values can be relied on. Do not claim age-gated or parent-managed accounts until the backend supplies and enforces that model.

## Reels publishing

The mobile `ReelsRemoteDataSource` contains feed reads, comments, likes, and profile reads only. There are no student create/upload/edit/delete controls or routes in the app. Ordinary users therefore cannot publish from this client. Backend authorization must independently reject unauthorized publishing, and the server contract should be reviewed before launch.

## AI English Tutor

- The voice tutor is labeled **AI English Tutor**.
- Each conversation view displays: “Responses are generated by AI, not by another user or teacher.”
- Every assistant message has an accessible “Report AI response” action with suggested reasons (inappropriate content, incorrect/misleading response, unsafe advice, other).
- No AI-report endpoint is exposed by the current backend client. The action does not pretend to submit; it explains the blocker and directs the user to support. Implement and wire a server endpoint before claiming in-product reporting is complete.

## Manual verification and screenshots

1. Open a Writing section, choose paper upload, verify the notice appears before the picker, test Cancel, outside tap, and back, then confirm the picker opens only after Continue.
2. Open profile editing, test both camera and gallery paths, and verify the avatar notice appears before either permission/picker prompt.
3. Open another user’s reel profile and verify “Report profile image” shows reasons and the honest unavailable message.
4. Open AI English Tutor, verify the title, disclosure, and “Report AI response” on assistant messages.
5. As an ordinary student, verify no Reels create/upload/edit/delete controls are present.

Capture screenshots of each notice, the avatar gate, the AI disclosure/report action, and the Reels profile without publishing controls for the Play Console review record.

## Claims that are safe now

It is safe to state that the app is an English-learning app, IELTS submissions are preceded by privacy guidance, avatar uploads are preceded by visibility/sensitivity guidance, students have no client-side Reels publishing controls, and AI responses are clearly labeled as AI-generated.

## Claims that are not safe yet

Do not claim verified adult-only access, parent-managed child accounts, complete server-side upload authorization, automatic metadata removal, or fully operational in-app profile/AI reporting until the backend contracts and enforcement are implemented and tested.

## Backend blockers

The backend contract still needs: explicit role/age/parent permission data, authoritative upload capabilities and enforcement, profile/avatar report endpoint, AI response report endpoint, and confirmation that Reels publishing is denied for ordinary users. This document records those gaps so they are not represented as completed features.
